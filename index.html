What it does

Every decision, with the reasoning attached.

The engine computes the numbers; the assistant only puts them into words. Nothing is ever guessed at.

◈

Lineup optimizer

Provably optimal lineups across FLEX, superflex and multi-position slots — with locks, byes and injury designations handled correctly.

◎

Waiver radar

Available players ranked by rest-of-season value under your league's scoring, not a generic default.

◐

Matchup preview

Win probability for the week and the handful of players whose games actually decide it.

⧉

Every league, one model

Yahoo and ESPN leagues normalized into a single view, scored under each league's exact rules.

✦

Explained, not hallucinated

Projections and probabilities come from a deterministic engine. The AI narrates them and cites the numbers it was given.

◉

Private by design

Runs on your own machine. Your credentials and league data never leave it — and nothing is written back to your league.

Data & privacy

What we read — and what we don't.

Access is scoped to leagues your own account belongs to, and it is read-only. That isn't a setting; it's the only mode there is.

  • ✓League settings & scoring rules — so projections match your league, not a default.
  • ✓Rosters, matchups & standings — for leagues you are a member of.
  • ✓Available players — the waiver pool, ranked under your rules.
  • ✕No writes. We never change a lineup, submit a claim, or send a message.
  • ✕No sharing. League data is never sold, aggregated, or used to train anything.
  • ✕No account creation. One person, one account, their own leagues.
